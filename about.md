## Who we are

<div class="person" markdown="1">
  <a id="fredericks" />
  <img class="headshot" src="{{ '/assets/img/people/fredericks-headshot-sm.jpg' | prepend: site.baseurl }}" />
  <h2 class="name"><a href="https://efredericks.github.io">Dr. Erik Fredericks</a></h2>
  <p class="desc"><b>(Primary Investigator)</b> Dr. Erik Fredericks an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Grand Valley State University.  His research focuses on exploring how uncertainty can impact self-adaptive systems at different levels of abstraction and how it can be mitigated by using search-based software engineering techniques.  Recent works focus on uncertainty applied to generative art, procedural content generation, and robotics.</p>
  <p class="desc">
    <span class="reu-badge"><a href="https://gvsu.edu/computing">GVSU &bull; College of Computing</a></span>

<div class="person" markdown="1">
  <a id="ferguson" />
  <img class="headshot" src="{{ '/assets/img/people/ferguson.jpg' | prepend: site.baseurl }}" />
  <h2 class="name"><a href="https://fergusonaj.github.io/">Dr. Austin Ferguson</a></h2>
  <p class="desc"><b>(Co-Primary Investigator)</b> Dr. Austin Ferguson is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Grand Valley State University. His research uses agent-based computational modeling to empirically investigate questions in evolutionary biology theory. His recent work has focused on historical contingency in evolution, evolvability, population structure, and the evolutionary dynamics of invasive species.</p>
  <p class="desc">
    <span class="reu-badge"><a href="https://gvsu.edu/computing">GVSU &bull; College of Computing</a></span>
  </p>

<div class="person" markdown="1">
  <a id="lalejini" />
  <img class="headshot" src="{{ '/assets/img/people/lalejini-2025.jpg' | prepend: site.baseurl }}" />
  <h2 class="name"><a href="https://lalejini.com/">Dr. Alexander Lalejini</a></h2>
  <p class="desc"><b>(Lead Mentor)</b> Dr. Alexander Lalejini is an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at Grand Valley State University. Dr. Lalejini's research intersects computer science and evolution, applying the principles of each field to advance the other. Broadly, his work focuses on (1) developing digital systems to investigate fundamental questions about how evolution works and (2) harnessing our understanding of evolution to engineer new search algorithms.</p>
  <p class="desc">
    <span class="reu-badge"><a href="https://gvsu.edu/computing">GVSU &bull; College of Computing</a></span>
  </p>
</div>

  <p class="desc">Dr. Stuart Jones is the Allen and Helen Hunting Executive Director of the Annis Water Resources Institute and an aquatic ecologist. He is happiest building simple mathematical models or at the hockey rink with his family. </p>
